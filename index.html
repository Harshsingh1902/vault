Act I  ·  The Lost Spark

A World Full of Noise

In a world that moved too fast and spoke too loud, there existed a traveler — neither searching for purpose, nor fleeing from one. He simply moved, as travelers do.



And then, in the chaos of lecture halls and corridors — he saw her. A small, quiet child, lost in the noise of the world. Not broken. Never broken. But unreached.



She carried within her a spark the world had not yet learned to see. It pulsed faintly — stubborn, patient — waiting for someone with the eyes to find it.

Act II  ·  The Guidance

The Traveler
Stepped In

He did not come to own her story. He did not come to be her destination. He came only to walk beside her — to hold a lantern where the road disappeared into shadow.



Like that beloved figure who crossed a border simply to return a child to where she belonged — the traveler's only mission was this: guide her safely through. Reunite her with her own strength. And then, quietly, let go.



The bond that formed was never designed by logic. It grew in the margins — between shared silences and unspoken understanding, between the chaos of examinations and the stillness of just being known.

Five utterly alien numbers, from five different mathematical universes, meet at a single perfect point — and dissolve into silence. This is Euler's Identity. Mathematicians have wept reading it.



We were that equation.



Most of the time, I find myself unfit for conventional relationships — a solitary creature by nature. But our alignment was a beautiful, rare anomaly against all mathematical odds. Two entirely different worlds, colliding perfectly for a fleeting moment, creating absolute peace.



Not forever. Not required to be. Perfect, precisely because it was finite.
